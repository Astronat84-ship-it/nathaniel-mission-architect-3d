How the physics works

Speed change: Δv = Isp × g₀ × ln(m₀ / m_f). In flight the game integrates real two-body orbital motion (Earth gravity) with your engine's thrust, and times the translunar burn to a launch window so the Moon is where your spacecraft arrives. Numbers are simplified and approximate; verify with NASA sources before quoting them.
